    [ no. 010 ] make your own

    Your photo, or your pet, in letters.

    Every print in the shop is a picture made of text. This one is yours. Build it in Coded Lens, the free Azoki tool that rebuilds any photo or clip out of glowing characters, then send it in to be printed.

    1. Open Coded Lens. Drop in a photo or a clip. It runs in your browser, so the picture stays on your device.
    2. Pick a look. Code Sea, Matrix Rain, Blueprint and seven more. Tune it until you like it.
    3. Export, then Print. It saves a poster-sized picture, up to 24 x 36 inches.
    4. Send it in. Email the file to hello@azokistudios.com with the size you want.

    Printing starts when the shop opens. Make yours now and shortlist one, and you will hear the day it does.
